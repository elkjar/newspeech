midi gate
1–4: singers. Octave is relative to D3. Out: internal samples or a MIDI port + channel; a slot whose port is unplugged plays its samples. Quad: the bird's quadrant (FL, FR, RL, RR) adds 0–3 to the channel. Midi gate = singer note length. Pad: the sampled bed (bed slider; 0 = off).
bed layers
chord
Each layer holds the current chord on a MIDI channel. Chord = four tones close · open = the same spread over two octaves · root = bass note · top = highest tone. Tones both chords share keep ringing. Chord: seconds per chord, or a new chord on every wave; strikes always jump. The sampled pad fades after ~4 s. Sweep: a slow rise and fall of one CC between the two percentages, once per period; PRO-800 targets are 14-bit.
