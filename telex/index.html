Telex

Short scripts typed on a keyboard. They play a small voice, sample instruments and MIDI out to the rest of the studio. Ten scripts a scene, a metro that runs one on a clock, four LFOs and four clocks that fire the others.

PROB 40: NOTE DEG RAND 14

Read from the right: each time it runs, a 40% chance of a random degree of the scale. The language is Monome Teletype's, cut down and pointed at MIDI. TELEX runs as custom firmware on an original Polyend Tracker, and the same firmware runs in a browser. Through a USB console a computer, Claude included, can read and rewrite the scripts while it keeps playing.

Readme

The field card: first five minutes, every word and mod, the rack, instruments, recipes, errors and scene files. Also as readme.txt.

Emulator

The box's firmware compiled to WebAssembly. The real screen, the real language, sound, and MIDI out to your gear.

Hardware

The desktop box TELEX should end up in: a Teensy 4.1, a 5″ screen, a USB keyboard, MIDI and audio. Diagrams, parts and cost.
