One of the biggest questions when starting this project revolves around my obsession with focusing in on a clear reason for existing. Why make this music? What message is it trying to convey? who is it for? why are you making it? This point of view feels difficult sometimes - this is even more complex when you remove the idea of lyrics carrying the meaning. My solution is as follows. Newspeech will release lyric videos the same way everyone else does - but ours will be the direct messaging of the concerns that caused the creation of this music. The guitar playing sounds like an alarm on this song because it fucking is.

## sources

Anyways, the sources are below. These links will send you out to the sites that I pulled them from. If you have a concern about my reading of any of this please feel free to reach out.

> IN THE U.S., FROM 1980 TO 2024,
> THERE WERE NINE BILLION-DOLLAR
> DISASTERS A YEAR, ON AVERAGE.

> THE LAST FIVE YEARS THERE HAVE
> BEEN MORE THAN TWENTY A YEAR.

- [NOAA NCEI, U.S. Billion-Dollar Weather and Climate Disasters, summary stats 1980–2024](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/billions/summary-stats/US/1980-2024) — inflation-adjusted (CPI). 1980–2024 average is 9 a year; 2020–2024 averaged 23 a year.

> 2024 WAS THE HOTTEST
> YEAR ON RECORD.
> 1.55°C ABOVE PRE-INDUSTRIAL.

- [WMO, "WMO confirms 2024 as warmest year on record at about 1.55°C above pre-industrial level"](https://wmo.int/news/media-centre/wmo-confirms-2024-warmest-year-record-about-155degc-above-pre-industrial-level) — consolidated from six international datasets, ±0.13°C, against the 1850–1900 baseline.

> IN 2024 ALONE THERE WERE
> TWENTY-SEVEN DISASTERS.

> HURRICANE HELENE: 250 DEAD.
> $78.7 BILLION IN DAMAGE.

> $182 BILLION IN DAMAGE
> IN A SINGLE YEAR.

> 568 AMERICANS DIED.

- [NOAA NCEI, U.S. Billion-Dollar Weather and Climate Disasters](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/billions/) — 2024: 27 separate billion-dollar events, $182.7 billion in damage, 568 deaths. US only. The 568 includes Helene's US deaths.
- [NOAA National Hurricane Center, Tropical Cyclone Report: Hurricane Helene (AL092024)](https://www.nhc.noaa.gov/data/tcr/AL092024_Helene.pdf) — "at least 250 fatalities in the United States (including at least 175 direct deaths)", the deadliest hurricane in the contiguous US since Katrina; an estimated $78.7 billion in US damage.

> IN 2025, THE U.S. GOVERNMENT
> STOPPED COUNTING THESE DISASTERS.

- [Scripps News, "NOAA says it will discontinue its billion-dollar disaster database"](https://scrippsnews.com/science-and-tech/climate-change/noaa-says-it-will-discontinue-its-billion-dollar-disaster-database) — announced May 8, 2025; 2024 is the last year NOAA published.
- [AP, "The federal government used to keep track of extreme weather disasters. Now it's up to a nonprofit"](https://krdo.com/news/2025/10/22/the-federal-government-used-to-keep-track-of-extreme-weather-disasters-now-its-up-to-a-nonprofit/) — in October 2025 the database's former NOAA lead, Adam Smith, relaunched it at [Climate Central](https://www.climatecentral.org/billion-dollar-disasters) using the same methods.

> HOME INSURANCE PREMIUMS ROSE
> BY A THIRD FROM 2020 TO 2023.

- [Keys & Mulder, "Property Insurance and Disaster Risk: New Evidence from Mortgage Escrow Data", NBER Working Paper 32579](https://www.nber.org/papers/w32579) — average homeowners premiums rose 33% (nominal) between 2020 and 2023. [Summary in the NBER Reporter](https://www.nber.org/reporter/2025number2/housing-climate-risk-and-insurance).

> INSURERS DECLINED TO RENEW
> NEARLY TWO MILLION HOME POLICIES,
> 2018–2023.

- [US Senate Budget Committee, "New data reveal climate change-driven insurance crisis is spreading"](https://www.budget.senate.gov/chairman/newsroom/press/new-data-reveal-climate-change-driven-insurance-crisis-is-spreading) — insurer-reported non-renewal data, December 2024. The committee published state-by-state data; the ~1.9 million total is the sum reported in coverage of the release.

> WORLDWIDE, INSURED DISASTER
> LOSSES REACHED $137 BILLION
> IN 2024.

- [Swiss Re Institute, sigma 1/2025: "Natural catastrophes: insured losses on trend to USD 145 billion in 2025"](https://www.swissre.com/institute/research/sigma-research/sigma-2025-01-natural-catastrophes-trend.html) — global insured natural-catastrophe losses of USD 137 billion in 2024.

> THE LOS ANGELES FIRES:
> $40 BILLION IN INSURED LOSSES.

- [Swiss Re Institute, "Wildfires, storms, floods contribute to record 92% of global insured losses in 2025"](https://www.swissre.com/press-release/Wildfires-storms-floods-contribute-to-record-92-of-global-insured-losses-in-2025-says-Swiss-Re-Institute/7b39b1a5-b878-4a55-a5ff-bf5aa561a675) — about USD 40 billion insured for the Palisades and Eaton fires, the largest insured wildfire loss on sigma's records. [Carbon Brief's summary](https://www.carbonbrief.org/how-wildfires-and-storms-drove-insurance-losses-in-2025-in-three-charts/).

> 31 PEOPLE DIED.
> MORE THAN 16,000
> BUILDINGS DESTROYED.

- [Cal Fire, Palisades Fire incident page](https://www.fire.ca.gov/incidents/2025/1/7/palisades-fire) — 12 civilian deaths, 6,845 structures destroyed.
- [Cal Fire, Eaton Fire incident page](https://www.fire.ca.gov/incidents/2025/1/7/eaton-fire) — 19 civilian deaths, 9,419 structures destroyed. Together: 31 dead, 16,264 structures (Cal Fire counts homes, businesses and outbuildings).

> EXTREME WEATHER DROVE FOOD PRICE
> SPIKES IN 18 COUNTRIES
> IN JUST TWO YEARS.

- [Kotz et al., "Climate extremes, food price spikes, and their wider societal risks", Environmental Research Letters 20 081001 (2025)](https://publications.pik-potsdam.de/rest/items/item_33452_1/component/file_33453/content) — 16 food price spikes in 18 countries, 2022–2024, each following heat, drought or heavy rain (e.g. onions and potatoes in India up more than 80% after the May 2024 heatwave, olive oil up 50% after drought in Spain and Italy, cocoa roughly tripling). The cases are prominent examples, not a complete count. [Carbon Brief's map of all 16](https://www.carbonbrief.org/mapped-16-times-extreme-weather-drove-higher-food-prices-since-2022/).

> IN 2024, DISASTERS FORCED
> PEOPLE FROM THEIR HOMES
> 45.8 MILLION TIMES. A RECORD.

> HURRICANES HELENE
> AND MILTON ALONE:
> 8.4 MILLION DISPLACEMENTS.

- [IDMC, Global Report on Internal Displacement 2025](https://www.internal-displacement.org/global-report/grid2025/) — 45.8 million disaster displacements in 2024, the highest on record; 99.5% from weather-related hazards; Helene and Milton together 8.4 million. These count displacements, not people: one person can be displaced more than once.

> PAKISTAN, 2022:
> FLOODS AFFECTED
> 33 MILLION PEOPLE.

> MORE THAN 1,700 DIED.
> ABOUT 8 MILLION WERE DISPLACED.

- [World Bank, Pakistan floods post-disaster needs assessment (October 2022)](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2022/10/28/pakistan-flood-damages-and-economic-losses-over-usd-30-billion-and-reconstruction-needs-over-usd-16-billion-new-assessme) — 33 million people affected, more than 1,700 killed, about 8 million displaced; over USD 30 billion in damage and economic losses.

> CLIMATE CHANGE ACCOUNTS FOR
> AN ESTIMATED $143 BILLION A YEAR
> IN EXTREME-WEATHER COSTS.

> NEARLY TWO-THIRDS OF THAT COST IS
> COUNTED IN HUMAN LIVES.

- [Newman & Noy, "The global costs of extreme weather that are attributable to climate change", Nature Communications (2023)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-023-41888-1) — USD 143 billion a year (2000–2019) of extreme-event costs attributable to climate change; 63% of it from loss of life.

> HEAT NOW KILLS AN ESTIMATED
> 546,000 PEOPLE A YEAR.

> 84% OF HEATWAVE DAYS
> WOULD NOT HAVE HAPPENED
> WITHOUT CLIMATE CHANGE.

- [The Lancet Countdown on Health and Climate Change, 2025 report](https://lancetcountdown.org/2025-report/) — an average of 546,000 heat-related deaths a year (2012–2021); 84% of heatwave days in 2020–2024 would not have occurred without climate change.

> HEAT KILLED 2,325 AMERICANS
> IN 2023. MORE THAN EVER RECORDED.

> U.S. HEAT DEATHS HAVE
> MORE THAN DOUBLED SINCE 1999.

- [Howard et al., "Trends of Heat-Related Deaths in the US, 1999–2023", JAMA (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2024.16386) — CDC death certificates: 1,069 heat-related deaths in 1999, 2,325 in 2023 (+117%), the highest in the series. The authors note death certificates likely undercount heat.

> DROUGHTS AND FLOODS PUSHED
> 96 MILLION PEOPLE INTO FOOD CRISIS
> IN 2024.

> 295 MILLION PEOPLE FACED
> ACUTE HUNGER IN 2024.
> THE SIXTH YEAR OF INCREASE.

- [Global Network Against Food Crises, Global Report on Food Crises 2025](https://www.fightfoodcrises.net/report/global-report-food-crises-2025/) — 295 million people in acute food insecurity in 2024, the sixth straight annual rise; weather extremes were the main driver for 96 million people in 18 countries. Conflict remains the largest driver overall.

> IN THE HORN OF AFRICA,
> CLIMATE CHANGE MADE DROUGHT
> AT LEAST 100 TIMES MORE LIKELY.

- [World Weather Attribution, "Human-induced climate change increased drought severity in Horn of Africa" (April 2023)](https://www.worldweatherattribution.org/human-induced-climate-change-increased-drought-severity-in-southern-horn-of-africa/) — the 2021–2022 agricultural drought was made at least 100 times more likely by climate change. [Imperial College summary](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/news/244671/without-climate-change-horn-africa-drought/).

> IN SOMALIA, AN ESTIMATED 43,000
> DIED IN 2022. HALF WERE
> CHILDREN UNDER FIVE.

- [UNICEF / WHO / Somalia Ministry of Health, "New study finds that 43,000 'excess deaths' may have occurred in 2022 from the drought in Somalia" (March 2023)](https://www.unicef.org/press-releases/new-study-finds-43000-excess-deaths-may-have-occurred-2022-drought-somalia) — a modelled estimate by the London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine and Imperial College London; about half were likely children under five.

> BY 2030, CLIMATE CHANGE
> COULD PUSH UP TO 132 MILLION
> PEOPLE INTO EXTREME POVERTY.

- [Jafino, Walsh, Rozenberg & Hallegatte, "Revised Estimates of the Impact of Climate Change on Extreme Poverty by 2030", World Bank Policy Research Working Paper 9417 (2020)](https://openknowledge.worldbank.org/handle/10986/34555) — 32 to 132 million more people in extreme poverty by 2030; 132 million is the top of the range. [World Bank blog summary](https://blogs.worldbank.org/climatechange/covid-climate-change-and-poverty-avoiding-worst-impacts).

> HEAT COST WORKERS $1.09 TRILLION
> IN LOST INCOME IN 2024.

- [The Lancet Countdown, 2025 report](https://lancetcountdown.org/2025-report/) — heat-related loss of labour capacity cost an estimated USD 1.09 trillion in potential income in 2024.

> WEATHER DISASTERS DISPLACED
> CHILDREN 43 MILLION TIMES
> IN SIX YEARS. 20,000 A DAY.

> RIVER FLOODS COULD DISPLACE
> 96 MILLION CHILDREN
> IN THE NEXT 30 YEARS.

- [UNICEF, "Children Displaced in a Changing Climate" (October 2023)](https://www.unicef.org/reports/children-displaced-changing-climate) — 43.1 million child displacements from floods, storms, drought and wildfire across 44 countries, 2016–2021, about 20,000 a day; riverine floods could displace nearly 96 million children over the next 30 years. [UN News summary](https://news.un.org/en/node/1141947).
