01 / vibe

vibe

v1.0.0 · free

based on my "always on" modular synth bed of delay, grains, reverb and glitches. vibe is three stages in series: a tape loop that grabs and mangles the incoming audio, a tempo-synced repeat that dices to your session bpm, and a big plate reverb to smear whatever survives.

put it on your master bus, probably.

02 / saturate

saturate

v1.0.0 · free

drive into a squelching filter, with a pll style micro-loop that grabs and repeats slices whenever the signal crosses a threshold. very tasteful. also features a distortion block that goes from gentle to basically square waves.

enjoy on everything.

download saturate for macos
6.4 mb zip · au + vst3 · source

install

unzip, then copy the .component to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components and the .vst3 to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3. restart your daw — logic may need a moment to rescan.

compatibility

au covers logic + garageband; vst3 covers ableton, reaper, bitwig, fl studio, studio one, cubase. no aax — pro tools users can wrap them with blue cat patchwork. signed + notarized, so macos opens them without complaint.
